Multi-Protocol Governance Audit Framework

Framework version 1.0.0. Six control areas (A1–A6), 23 checks, weights sum to 100. Area score is the mean of its checks (pass 1.0, partial 0.5, fail 0.0); protocol score is the weighted sum. Every parameter is verified against a primary source; unverified values are scored partial or fail, never invented.

Claims

  1. C-01 — Compound proposal threshold is 25,000 COMP delegated: Addresses delegated at least 25,000 COMP can create a Compound governance proposal; any address can lock 100 COMP for an Autonomous Proposal that becomes a proposal after 25,000 COMP is delegated.
  2. C-02 — Compound quorum is 400,000 COMP for-votes plus a majority: A Compound proposal passes only if a majority votes for it AND at least 400,000 COMP (4% of the 10M supply) is cast for it; only for-votes count toward quorum.
  3. C-03 — Compound lifecycle is 2-day review, 3-day vote, 2-day timelock, 14-day grace: Compound proposals enter a 2-day review period (13,140 blocks), then a 3-day voting period; passing proposals are queued in the Timelock and can be implemented 2 days later, within a 14-day grace period.
  4. C-04 — Compound Proposal 289 passed narrowly and moved 499,000 COMP: Proposal 289 passed on 2024-07-28 with 682,191 for vs 633,636 against (51.9%), moving 499,000 COMP (about $24-25M) from the treasury to a group-controlled goldCOMP treasury the DAO could not recall; the proposers agreed to rescind it on 2024-07-30.
  5. C-05 — Uniswap proposal threshold is 1M UNI today (2.5M originally): The current Uniswap process (Jan 2023 update) requires at least 1M UNI delegated to submit an on-chain proposal; the original process required 2.5M UNI (0.25% of supply).
  6. C-06 — Uniswap quorum is 40M UNI for-votes: A Uniswap proposal passes only with at least 40M UNI (4% of the 1B supply) voting for it; only for-votes count toward quorum.
  7. C-07 — Uniswap voting period is stated as both 6 and 7 days in one primary source: The Jan 2023 Community Governance Process Update states a "6 day voting period" in its Phase 3 timeframe line and "a seven day voting period begins" in its body text — an unresolved discrepancy inside a single primary source.
  8. C-08 — Uniswap governance contracts are Timelock 0x1a9C…35BC and GovernorBravo 0x408E…24C3: The official Uniswap technical reference lists Timelock 0x1a9C8182C09F50C8318d769245beA52c32BE35BC and GovernorBravo 0x408ED6354d4973f66138C91495F2f2FCbd8724C3 as the active Ethereum mainnet governance contracts.
  9. C-09 — Uniswap UNIfication proposal passed 125,342,017 for vs 742 against: The UNIfication proposal was put on-chain 2025-12-18, voted 2025-12-19 to 2025-12-25, and passed with 125,342,017 UNI for vs 742 against (above the 40M quorum); it activates the protocol fee switch and a one-time burn of 100M UNI after the 2-day timelock.
  10. C-10 — Aave Level 1 is 1-day delay, 3-day vote, 1-day timelock, 7-day grace: Aave Governance V3 Short Executor (Level 1) proposals run a 1-day voting delay, a 3-day voting period, a 1-day timelock, and a 7-day grace period.
  11. C-11 — Aave quorum is 320,000 votes and the differential is 80,000: An Aave proposal succeeds only if at least 320,000 AAVE/stkAAVE/aAAVE participate AND the winning option receives at least 320,000 votes; per the ARFC framework, YAE votes must exceed the second-largest option by at least 80,000.
  12. C-12 — Aave Level 2 is a 10-day vote and 7-day timelock with unstated exact quorum: Aave Long Executor (Level 2) proposals run a 10-day voting period and a 7-day timelock with a higher quorum and vote differential, but the current official docs do not state the exact quorum or differential values.
  13. C-13 — Aave has a 4-of-7 Protocol Emergency Guardian and a 5-of-9 Governance Emergency Guardian: The Protocol Emergency Guardian is a 4-of-7 multisig (signers published in the docs) that can pause markets; the Governance Emergency Guardian is a 5-of-9 multisig (signers published) that can cancel malicious or erroneous proposals.
  14. C-14 — Aave Level 2 update was approved on Snapshot with 325,000 AAVE: The 2022 Level 2 requirements update was approved on Snapshot with 325,000 AAVE votes and implemented via on-chain proposals created 2022-10-04 through an Autonomous Proposal contract requiring 320,000 AAVE proposition delegation.
  15. C-15 — Engine scores are Aave 92.50, Compound 86.13, Uniswap 75.88: The audit engine scores Aave 92.50, Compound 86.13, and Uniswap 75.88 out of 100 across the six weighted control areas.
  16. C-16 — The engine emits 17 findings: 1 High, 8 Medium, 8 Low: Across the three audits the engine emits 17 findings — 1 High (Compound F-01), 8 Medium, 8 Low — each tied to a rubric check outcome.
  17. C-17 — A clean re-run from a fresh copy is byte-identical: Running `bash scripts/clean_reproduce.sh` re-runs the audit from a fresh copy of the pack into outputs/run2-clean and reports MATCH — outputs/run1 and outputs/run2-clean are byte-identical.
  18. C-18 — Digest verification reports all OK: Running `bash scripts/verify_digests.sh` checks SHA256SUMS over every static pack file and reports all OK.

Reproduce
